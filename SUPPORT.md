# Support and Updates

Vibe96 Seller Agreement version 3.1 currently requires sellers to publish defect/security updates for 12 months after each sale and answer buyer support tickets within two business days for 30 days. These duties apply only if the owner reviews and signs the agreement during submission; this source package does not sign or accept it on the owner's behalf.

Buyers should open support requests through the support ticket area attached to their Vibe96 purchase. This keeps the request, license, response history, and any approved update in the marketplace workspace. Never post credentials or customer data in a ticket.

Custom features, third-party service charges, hosting administration, data migration, and buyer-modified code are outside included support and may be quoted separately.

Configure `NEXT_PUBLIC_SUPPORT_URL` with the final HTTPS help-desk or seller contact page. Marketplace support should remain in the Vibe96 ticket area when required by the signed agreement. Never send passwords, API keys, database credentials, or production customer data in a support ticket.
