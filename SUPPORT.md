# Support and Updates

The Vibe96 purchase includes 30 days of product support. Support requests will receive a response within two business days. Support covers installation guidance, reproducible defects in the delivered version, and clarification of documented features.

Custom features, third-party service charges, hosting administration, data migration, and buyer-modified code are outside included support and may be quoted separately.

Buyers receive compatible product updates published during the 12 months following purchase. Security fixes take priority. Never send passwords, API keys, database credentials, or production customer data in a support ticket.
